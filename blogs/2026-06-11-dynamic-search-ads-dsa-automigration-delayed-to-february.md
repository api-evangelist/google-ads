What is changing? Google is extending the timeline for the transition of Dynamic Search Ads (DSA) to AI Max for Search campaigns and restoring campaign creation functionality. Automigration Delayed: The automatic upgrade of DSA campaigns to AI Max (or Search campaigns with broad match and Smart Bidding) has been postponed from September 2026 to February 2027 .
